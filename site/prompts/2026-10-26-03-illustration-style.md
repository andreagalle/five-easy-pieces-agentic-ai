**Context:** the author brought a complete system prompt for the header images, and a recurring cast with one subject per article. It gave the series its visual identity: a small cleaner fish in a glass sphere whose wall thickens from piece to piece. Journal: [session 03]({{ "/journal/2026-10-26-03-illustrations/" | relative_url }}).

The prompt is kept verbatim in the repository, so here it is only summarized:

- **Style:** flat, mid-century-modern vector illustration with a risograph grain; no outlines, gradients or 3D.
- **World:** a calm, deep sea with shafts of light, kelp, fan corals and bubbles. The mood is "wonder, not drama".
- **Palette:** plum-navy, teals, turquoise, cerulean, seafoam, cream; coral and orange as accents only.
- **Concepts as metaphors:** never draw computers, screens, code or diagrams, and never any text.
- **Cast:** the cleaner (the agent), the sphere (the sandbox), the whale (the cloud, only in 00 and 06), the moon (the critic, only in 05).

Full text: `illustrations/style.txt`, `illustrations/cast.txt` and `illustrations/subjects/`.

Why it matters: the images now carry the same argument as the articles. The sandbox goes from a formality to the thing that makes the agent safe, and you can see it in the glass.
