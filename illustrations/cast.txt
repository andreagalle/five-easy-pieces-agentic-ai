RECURRING CAST (when the subject mentions them, draw them exactly like this in every image)
- The cleaner: a small, slender cleaner fish, cerulean blue with one dark plum-navy stripe from snout to tail and a pale cream belly, with a simple almond-shaped eye. It is always small relative to the scene.
- The sphere: a clear glass globe resting on the seabed. The glass shows only as a faint cream rim highlight and a slight teal tint. Inside it is a square patch of seabed laid out as a 4×4 grid of flat sand-colored stones, some marked with dark specks of debris.
- The whale: a large, calm whale drawn as a natural animal, usually far away or high above, partly in silhouette.
- The moon: a pale cream full moon above the water's surface, with a soft halo.
