A split view at the waterline at night. On the calm surface floats a small wooden boat with a single small figure bent over an open logbook, lit by a lantern, and a second, softer lantern glows beside it like a quiet companion. Below the waterline, the five spheres rest in a gentle arc on the seabed, each with its cleaner, and the whale swims slowly between the boat and the spheres, linking the two worlds.
