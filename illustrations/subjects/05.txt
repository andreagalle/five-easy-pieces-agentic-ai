At night, the sealed sphere rests on the seabed. Inside, the cleaner follows a new, smoother path, with faint ghost trails of its earlier, clumsier paths fading behind it. Far above, beyond the surface, the moon watches, out of reach. A small glass bottle holding a blank rolled note drifts upward from a hatch in the sphere toward the surface, and another drifts back down: the only way anything passes between them.
